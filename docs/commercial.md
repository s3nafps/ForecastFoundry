# Commercial boundaries

The Apache-2.0 community edition includes the domain contracts, public-provider
adapters, deterministic models and calibration, paper ledger, risk and
reconciliation controls, CLI, REST/OpenAPI, MCP server, Docker deployment,
tests, and fixtures.

Paid hosted or enterprise services may add managed deployment, upgrades,
backups, monitoring, tenant isolation, SSO/RBAC, retention and audit exports,
licensed premium data, support/SLA, calibration reports, and commercial
embedding or redistribution licenses. Safety-critical risk and execution code
remains auditable in the community edition.

ForecastFoundry does not guarantee profit or performance, provide investment
advice, pool capital, or take custody of customer funds. Any future custody,
managed execution, or revenue-share product requires separate jurisdictional
legal and compliance review.
